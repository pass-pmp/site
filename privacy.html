PassPMP Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

PassPMP is a self-study app for the PMP certification exam. It was built privacy-first: the app does not collect your personal information.

What the app stores

Your study progress, answers, scores, and settings are stored only on your device. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Network connections

The app connects to our content host (Cloudflare R2) only to stream or download the audio recordings for questions and explanations. Your device sends no personal information with those requests beyond the standard technical data any web connection carries (such as your IP address), which the hosting provider may record in server logs under its own privacy policy.

The public demo website

The free web demo of PassPMP is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic technical information about visitors under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The demo stores your progress in your own browser only.

Children

PassPMP is a professional exam study tool and is not directed at children under 13.
