Common questions

Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. PassPMP has no accounts and collects no personal data. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does PassPMP work offline?
Yes. Questions you have opened are stored on the device, and audio can be downloaded for offline study. A connection is needed the first time you stream a recording.

An audio recording will not play. What do I do?
Check your connection, then close and reopen the question. If a specific recording still fails, email us the question ID shown on screen and we will fix it.

Is PassPMP affiliated with PMI?
No. PassPMP is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Project Management Institute (PMI). PMP is a registered mark of the Project Management Institute, Inc. All questions in PassPMP are original and written for this app.

How do I report a wrong answer or a bad question?
Email us the question ID and what looks off. Every report gets read and the bank gets corrected in an update.
